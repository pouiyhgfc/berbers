An Introduction to Tarifiyt Berber

Khalid Mourigh & Maarten Kossmann (2019). The academic source that all explanations and lessons on this site are based on.

The book describes the Tarifit of Nador (Iqeṛɛiyen / Azghenghan) systematically — from sounds to sentence structure. It is written for linguists, so formal — but every phenomenon is illustrated with examples.
